Will my games still be here next time?
Yes. There's no account or log-in, so your games, artwork and saves are stored in this browser on this device. They stay there when you close the app, sign out of other things or restart the device.

They will be lost if you clear this browser's data or uninstall the browser. They also won't appear in a private/incognito window, a different browser or another device. On iPhone and iPad, Safari can clear website data after a few weeks unused, unless you add Easy Arcade to your home screen.

⚖️ About & legal

Easy Arcade is a front end for open-source emulators. It contains no games.

Built on EmulatorJS (GPL-3.0) with the FinalBurn Neo and MAME 2003-Plus engines, used under their non-commercial licences.
